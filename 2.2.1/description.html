Hide & Seek

A multiplayer gamemode. One player is the seeker and sits behind a black screen for 30 seconds while everyone else scatters across the track. When the timer runs out the seeker is released and has to drive into the hiders.

Everyone in the lobby needs this mod. Players without it are left as spectators and never see the round.

Modes

  • Hide & Seek — a caught hider is eliminated. The last hider standing wins; if the clock runs out, the survivors win.
  • Infection — a caught hider joins the seekers. Seekers win by catching everybody, hiders win on the clock.
  • Tag — whoever gets caught becomes the new IT. At the end the player who spent the least time as IT wins.

Playing

  1. Go to Multiplayer → Host. The three modes appear in the Game Mode row, next to Casual and Competitive.
  2. Pick one, choose a track and host. The round starts on its own once at least two players with the mod have joined.
  3. The host can also press N at any time to start or stop a round by hand, including during a plain Casual or Competitive session, and B (or the ↻ New seeker button) to redraw the seeker without changing track. Only the host can do this.
  4. Hiding time, round length and the rest live under Settings → Hide & Seek on the host's machine. Those settings only matter for whoever is hosting.

A session hosted in one of these modes is still a Casual session as far as PolyTrack itself is concerned — the gamemode rides along on the mod's own channel, so joining works exactly as it always did.

What changes while a round is running

  • Skid marks and name tags are switched off — both of them lead straight to a hider.
  • The seeker is blindfolded, frozen and muted for the hiding phase, so there is nothing to see and no engines to listen for.
  • Hiders get a red glow around the screen when a seeker is close.
  • In the last third of the round the seeker gets a compass bearing to the nearest hider, so rounds actually end.
  • Catches have a short cooldown, and anyone who just respawned is safe for a moment so the start line cannot be camped.
  • Eliminated players keep driving as translucent ghosts and can neither catch nor be caught, and can press V to ride along with any other player's camera. A seeker never can.
  • Hiders freeze 15 seconds after the seeker is released, so you have to commit to a hiding place. The car still settles and rolls — the pedals just stop doing anything.
  • Touching the grass, driving out into the mountains, falling off the map or landing on your roof puts you back on the start line.
  • Each new round moves the lobby to a random community track.

A big open track works far better than a narrow circuit — community tracks with plenty of side routes and dead ends are ideal.
